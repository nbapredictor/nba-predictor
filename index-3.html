How it works

Every team has a rating that goes up after wins and down after losses, built from every NBA game since 2015. The model then adjusts for rest and back-to-backs, for key players listed as out, and for each team's current roster. It was trained on past seasons and tested on seasons it had never seen: in those tests it picked about two out of three winners, and its probabilities matched how often teams actually won. Data comes from ESPN. Built in Python.

For fun, not betting advice. Percentages are estimates, not guarantees, and the NBA is full of upsets. Early numbers are noisy until about 100 games have been tracked.
